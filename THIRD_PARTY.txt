CookieBot's object detector uses:

- ONNX Runtime 1.24.4 (onnxruntime.dll, onnxruntime_providers_shared.dll) - MIT License, Microsoft. https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
- DirectML (DirectML.dll) - redistributable under the Microsoft DirectML license, shipped in the onnxruntime-directml package.
- YOLOX-S (yolox_s.onnx) - Apache License 2.0, Megvii. https://github.com/Megvii-BaseDetection/YOLOX

The .dl_ files are the DLLs renamed; CookieBot downloads them next to itself and names them .dll.
